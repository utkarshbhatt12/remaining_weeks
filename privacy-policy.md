# Privacy Policy for Remaining Weeks Chrome Extension

## Introduction

The **Remaining Weeks** Chrome extension respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This document outlines how the extension collects, uses, and stores your data.

## Data Collection

The extension collects the following information:

- **Name**: Provided by the user.

- **Date of Birth**: Provided by the user.

This information is collected solely for the purpose of calculating and displaying the remaining weeks in your life.

## Data Storage

The collected data is stored locally on your device using **Chrome.storage**. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.

## Data Usage

The collected data is used exclusively within the extension to:

- Display a personalized timeline of your remaining weeks.

## Data Sharing

The extension does not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties.

## Security

The extension uses Chrome's secure storage mechanisms to ensure your data is protected. However, the security of your data also depends on the security of your device.

## Your Consent

By using the **Remaining Weeks** Chrome extension, you consent to the collection and use of your data as outlined in this privacy policy.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be updated in this document. Please review it periodically to stay informed.
